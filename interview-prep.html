01 · Mental model

The project is about the evaluator

A simulator produces behavior. An evaluator decides which parts of that behavior matter. EvalSim makes that decision visible by using simple probes with known weaknesses and refusing to hide disagreement behind one score.

Question

Do trajectory fidelity, geometric interaction, and fixed-step feasibility produce the same ordering?

不同 evaluator 会不会对同一组 simulation 给出相同结论？

Method

Run log replay, constant velocity, and EvalSim IDM on the same source scenes; preserve each metric separately.

同场景、同 horizon、paired comparison，不做 composite realism score。

Insight

Metric choice encodes a value judgment. Fidelity, interaction proxy, and feasibility can disagree without any implementation bug.

冲突不是必须消掉的噪声；它可能正是 decision trade-off。

Interview framing: Lead with the question and the mixed evidence. Mention “personal learning project / work in progress” immediately after the contribution, not instead of it.
Say it aloud: “The contribution is the evaluation methodology, not a production simulator.” Then explain one reason why a single realism score would be misleading.

02 · Minimum domain knowledge

Bilingual glossary

You do not need broad AV expertise to explain this project. You do need exact meanings for the objects, comparison logic, and claim boundaries below.

20 terms
AV · Autonomous Vehicle
自动驾驶车辆；本项目关注 traffic-agent trajectory evaluation，而不是 sensor rendering。
Ego · 自车 / 焦点车辆
The designated focal vehicle. In accepted M5, ego follows its logged trajectory exogenously while policies simulate world agents.
World agent · 其他交通参与者
A non-ego vehicle, pedestrian, or cyclist whose future state may be simulated.
Scenario · 场景合同
Substrate-neutral input containing history/current state, reference future, validity, identity, geometry, and metadata.
Rollout · 模拟输出
A policy’s future trajectories plus simulator identity, version, seed, perturbation, and provenance.
Current frame · 当前帧
current_index is the last observed frame. Simulation starts with the next transition.
Metric · 诊断指标
A versioned function with units, direction, eligibility, invalid reasons, and retained component values—not merely one floating-point score.
Construct · 构念
The concept a metric intends to observe. Construct validity asks whether it responds to that concept for the intended reason.
Eligibility & missingness · 适用性与缺失
Which components/scenarios a metric can validly score, and why any value is unavailable. Missing is not automatically zero.
Paired comparison · 配对比较
Compare two policies within the same scenario so scenario-mix differences do not drive the contrast.
Resampling unit · 重采样单位
The scenario, not an agent or frame, because components within a scene are correlated.
Source-only slice · 源数据切片
A cohort subgroup defined from Scenario before policy output or metric outcomes are known.
Privileged · 使用未来信息
A method can access information unavailable to a causal simulator. Log replay is privileged because it copies the recorded future.
Closed loop · 闭环
In a closed-loop path, future actions observe simulated current state. M5 CV and IDM world-agent paths do this; privileged log replay instead copies the recorded future. Ego remains logged/exogenous for every accepted M5 baseline, so full ego-conditioned reactivity is not demonstrated.
IDM · Intelligent Driver Model
A longitudinal car-following model. EvalSim’s implementation responds to bumper gap but lacks lateral control and route-aware leader selection.
WOMD
Waymo Open Motion Dataset. EvalSim uses a licensed, local-only path; no raw or processed dataset is published.
Waymax
An optional licensed data/execution/reference adapter behind EvalSim contracts; useful for bounded cross-checks, not independent ground truth.
JAX
A numerical computing system used here for bounded jit/vmap CPU evidence. It is not required by the core evaluator and does not imply GPU scale.
Metric field of view · 指标视野
The variables a metric can observe by construction. A position metric cannot detect a velocity-only change if position stays fixed.
Generator artifact · 生成器伪影
A conclusion caused by an incorrect defect injector rather than by the evaluator behavior being studied.

Source / adapter

Produces the same contract from analytic synthetic scenes or licensed WOMD records.

Why: evaluator logic should not know the storage format.

Scenario

Validates timestamps, trajectories, masks, agent identities, dimensions, ego, map geometry, and metadata.

Why: one neutral semantic boundary prevents representation leakage.

Simulator policy

Consumes observed/current state and returns typed controls or an explicitly privileged state override.

Why: each probe isolates a legible behavior assumption.

Rollout engine

Owns timestamps, validity masks, births/re-entries, synchronous updates, physical integration, and feasibility clamps.

Why: policies cannot silently mutate time or lifecycle semantics.

Rollout

Records generated series plus sim name/version, seed, perturbation, and metadata.

Why: a number without execution identity is not reproducible evidence.

Metric contract

Declares construct, version, unit, direction, eligibility, missing reasons, scalar result, and retained components.

Why: missingness and distributions must survive aggregation.

Slices + paired statistics

Defines groups before outcomes, reduces to one value per scenario, then compares policies within each scenario.

Why: prevents outcome-dependent grouping and frame-level pseudo-replication.

Evidence + stress audit

Immutable manifests bind the official result; analytic oracles and M7 defects test calculations and field of view.

Why: correctness, construct validity, and decision relevance require different evidence.

Two supporting paths: Synthetic cases provide analytic oracles. Waymax provides optional licensed data/reference checks. Neither is allowed to bypass Scenario → Rollout → Metric.

04 · Comparison probes

Three baselines with known failure modes

These are not three equivalent production simulators. Their simplicity is experimental leverage: when the output differs, you can reason about why.

Privileged construction oracle

Log replay

Copies the exact recorded future for world agents.

  • Perfect logged-reference error by construction.
  • Useful for mapping and metric oracles.
  • Deterministic and easy to audit.

Known limitation: sees the future and cannot react to a changed ego plan. It is not a causal deployment candidate or realism upper bound.

Minimal causal extrapolator

Constant velocity · CV

Continues each world agent’s current world-frame velocity.

  • History-only.
  • Tests how far simple inertia goes.
  • Creates a transparent no-interaction reference.

Known limitation: no interaction, acceleration intent, map following, or route reasoning.

Limited interaction probe

EvalSim IDM

Intelligent Driver Model: longitudinal bumper-gap response.

  • History-only.
  • Can accelerate or brake in response to a selected leader.
  • Tests whether simple longitudinal interaction changes evaluation.

Known limitation: no lateral controller; geometric, non-route-aware leader selection; pedestrians/cyclists fall back to CV.

Do not say: “IDM is the realistic baseline.” Its extra interaction assumption can improve one construct while worsening recorded-trajectory fidelity. That trade-off is the experiment.

06 · Experimental reasoning

What we did, step by step

The goal was not merely to run policies. Each step removes a specific alternative explanation for the result.

Freeze the question and definitions before outcomes

Pre-register metric versions, primary cells, comparison direction, cohort rules, missingness, stability procedure, and claim gates.

Resolves: outcome-driven metric selection and silent definition changes.

Build one deterministic conditional cohort

From WOMD validation shards 00000–00009, account for 2,916 raw records, 1,527 eligible records, and select 128 complete cases without quota deficit or fallback.

Resolves: hidden selection and untraceable cohort drift—not representativeness.

Define slices from source only

Compute eight slice memberships from Scenario before policy identity, rollout, or metric value is available.

Resolves: policy-dependent subgroup membership.

Run every probe on the same scenes

Execute log replay, CV, and IDM deterministically with seed 0 over the same 128 scenarios and 80 post-current transitions; ego remains logged/exogenous.

Resolves: scenario-mix confounding between policies.

Compute typed component results

Evaluate 13 registered metrics while retaining units, directions, validity, missing reasons, eligibility counts, and component distributions.

Resolves: an opaque score that cannot be audited.

Reduce once per scenario

Aggregate eligible agent/frame components to one scenario-level scalar for each policy and metric.

Resolves: treating correlated frames or agents as independent samples.

Form paired policy effects

For each metric, compare A−B within a scenario. All 12 primary all-slice cells retain paired n=128.

Resolves: between-scene variation obscuring policy contrasts.

Measure finite-cohort stability

Use deterministic scenario-level empirical reweighting: 100,000 resamples for each primary cell, with pointwise and multiplicity-adjusted bands.

Resolves: whether the observed fixed-cohort mean depends on a small empirical subset. It does not estimate population sampling error.

Retain nulls, sparse cells, and contradictions

Keep all 12 primary cells, including the two that do not pass the adjusted directional gate; suppress claims for sparse/insufficient evidence.

Resolves: winner-by-selective-reporting.

Cross-check semantics without calling the reference truth

On a bounded 16-scene × 20-transition subset, compare three overlapping metrics to native Waymax semantics; observed gates pass.

Resolves: mapping/computation mistakes in that bounded domain—not construct validity or decoder independence.

1 · Implementation correctness

Was the metric computed as specified?

Evidence: analytic oracles, contracts, reconstruction, bounded parity.

2 · Construct validity

Does it respond to the intended behavior for the intended reason?

Evidence: M7 adds narrow field-of-view and generator-audit evidence.

3 · Decision relevance

Does that construct answer the downstream AV question?

Status: task-specific; accepted M5 does not establish counterfactual reactivity or safety.

What we resolved

  • The metric implementation and official row domains are auditable.
  • Distinct evaluator families disagree on the fixed cohort.
  • Ten of twelve adjusted fixed-cohort bands exclude zero.
  • The two unsupported contrasts remain visible.
  • A single overall winner is not supported.

What we did not resolve

  • Which simulator is universally “most realistic.”
  • WOMD-population or deployment-distribution performance.
  • Causal ego-conditioned reactivity.
  • Safety or real-vehicle validity.
  • Stochastic-policy uncertainty.
  • Whether a fixed-step diagnostic is complete physical feasibility.
Bounded conclusion: On the fixed 128-scene complete-case conditional cohort, no baseline is universally best. Replay best reproduces recorded trajectories; CV matches the log better than IDM among the history-only probes; and CV and IDM have lower fixed-step kinematic-infeasibility rates than replay. IDM has no supported overall advantage over CV.

Statistical boundary: The bands summarize sensitivity to empirical scene reweighting inside this fixed cohort. They are not confidence intervals, p-values, hypothesis tests, or WOMD-population uncertainty.

The bug

Freeze v1 rewrote the current frame

It zeroed velocity beginning at current_index, the last observed frame. That altered history and erased the current-to-future deceleration the kinematic metric should have seen.

The correction

Freeze v2 starts in the future

It preserves every field through the current frame, then holds future position/heading and sets future velocity to zero. The abrupt stop is now visible to the fixed-step diagnostic.

Permitted claim: On three hand-built analytic cases, the probes produced the complete six-response/six-non-response matrix. This verifies intended field of view and complementary blind spots: position misses velocity-only corruption, the fixed-step diagnostic misses position-only corruption, and overlap responds only to interpenetration in these cases.
Always add: This audit is outcome-aware. It is not calibrated, source-disjoint, held-out, WOMD-backed, population-level, or general metric validation. It does not evaluate speed_error_mps, prove generic nonreactivity detection, or satisfy the original M7 v1 gates.

09 · Tell the story

30 seconds, 2 minutes, 10 minutes

Use the short version first and expand only when the interviewer signals interest. Every version leads with the question, gives evidence, and ends with a bounded insight.

Target: 30–40 seconds

EvalSim asks whether evaluator choice changes how we judge AV traffic simulations. I ran privileged log replay, constant velocity, and a limited longitudinal IDM on the same 128-scene conditional cohort. Four diagnostics disagreed: replay best matched recorded position and speed and had the lowest overlap mean, while CV and IDM had lower fixed-step kinematic-infeasibility rates. So the evidence supports no overall winner; simulator evaluation must stay metric- and use-case-specific. It is a personal learning project: core M5 is complete, while broader validation remains work in progress.

Target: 1:45–2:15

EvalSim is a metrics-first learning project. Instead of asking which simulator is universally most realistic, I asked whether different evaluators produce one consistent ordering on the same scenes.

I chose three intentionally simple probes. Log replay copies the recorded future, so it is a privileged construction oracle and cannot react. Constant velocity is a minimal history-only causal extrapolator with no map or interaction reasoning. EvalSim IDM adds longitudinal bumper-gap response, but no lateral controller or route-aware leader selection.

All three ran on the same fixed 128-scene complete-case conditional WOMD cohort. I reduced valid agent-frame components to one value per scenario and used the scenario as the paired comparison and resampling unit. Slice membership was defined from source data before outcomes were available.

The four lower-is-better diagnostics disagreed. Replay had the best recorded-position and speed fidelity and the lowest overlap mean. CV and IDM had lower fixed-step kinematic-infeasibility rates than replay. CV matched the log better than IDM, while the IDM-versus-CV overlap and kinematic differences did not support directional claims.

Therefore I did not create a composite realism score or declare a winner. M7 then injected controlled defects and exposed complementary metric blind spots. It also caught a flawed freeze generator, which taught me that evaluating an evaluator requires validating the corruption generator too.

10 · Adversarial drill

Answer the hard follow-ups

Answer aloud before opening each card. Strong answers are precise, bounded, and decision-oriented; they do not apologize for an honest limitation.

Random interview question

Press “Draw a question,” answer aloud, then reveal.

Are you comparing which simulator is most realistic? 你是在找最真实的模拟器吗？Q01

Bounded answer · English

No. I compare how distinct diagnostics judge the same three probes on one fixed conditional cohort. The experiment has no independent scalar ground truth for “realism,” and the metrics encode different downstream preferences. The accepted result supports a multi-metric method, not a universal ranking.

Coach: 先纠正问题定义，再给 evidence boundary。

Why use such simple baselines? 为什么不用更强的 simulator？Q02

Bounded answer · English

Simplicity is experimental leverage. Replay, CV, and limited IDM have legible assumptions and failure modes, so I can attribute metric changes to specific behavior differences. A more complex model might score better, but it would make evaluator behavior harder to understand and would answer a different question.

Coach: 不要把简单说成资源不足；强调 identification。

Why not combine the metrics into one realism score? 为什么不做总分？Q03

Bounded answer · English

A weighted score would hide the central result: the metric families disagree. The weights would encode an unstated product decision about the value of fidelity, interpenetration, and fixed-step feasibility. I would only aggregate after a specific use case supplies defensible utilities and guardrails; this experiment does not.

Coach: “No composite” 是 deliberate governance choice。

Did log replay win? Replay 是 winner 吗？Q04

Bounded answer · English

Replay won the logged-position and logged-speed fidelity comparisons by construction and had the lowest overlap mean on this cohort. But it uses the recorded future and cannot react, so it is a privileged construction oracle rather than a causal simulator. It cannot be called the overall winner or a realism upper bound.

Coach: 区分 score outcome 与 method capability。

Why can replay look worse on kinematic infeasibility? 真实记录怎么会更“不合理”？Q05

Bounded answer · English

The metric applies pinned inverse-dynamics thresholds under a fixed 0.1-second semantic to discrete states. It can respond to sampled or noisy logged transitions differently from engine-integrated baselines. The result demonstrates a diagnostic tension; it does not prove the recorded motion is physically impossible or that CV and IDM are safer.

Coach: 不要反推 “data bad” 或 “policy safe”。

Why is CV closer to the log than IDM? 有 interaction 的 IDM 为什么更差？Q06

Bounded answer · English

On this cohort and implementation, adding limited longitudinal bumper-gap response makes trajectories diverge more from the single recorded future than simple inertia does. IDM also lacks lateral and route-aware control. That says CV has better logged fidelity here; it does not establish that ignoring interaction is generally more realistic.

Coach: metric-specific result，不做 general model judgment。

Why is the scenario the statistical unit? 为什么不是 frame 或 agent？Q07

Bounded answer · English

Frames and agents within one traffic scene share context and are strongly dependent. Treating them as independent would create pseudo-replication and overly narrow uncertainty summaries. I first reduce eligible components to one scalar per scenario, then form paired policy effects and reweight scenarios.

Coach: 说清 aggregation unit 与 resampling unit。

Are the stability bands confidence intervals? 这些 band 有统计显著性吗？Q08

Bounded answer · English

No. They are deterministic empirical scene-reweighting summaries for the fixed cohort. They show whether a mean contrast is sensitive to which observed scenes receive more weight. They are not sampling-error confidence intervals, p-values, hypothesis tests, or population-level familywise guarantees.

Coach: 这是最重要的 statistical boundary。

Does the 128-scene cohort represent WOMD? 能泛化到 WOMD 吗？Q09

Bounded answer · English

No. It is a deterministic complete-case conditional cohort selected from the first ten validation shards after a frozen eligibility predicate. It supports a bounded application and paired comparison on those scenes, not WOMD prevalence, probability-sample inference, geography generalization, or deployment performance.

Coach: “conditional” 必须主动说。

Is overlap a safety metric? Overlap 是否代表 collision/safety？Q10

Bounded answer · English

No. It is a target-frame oriented-box interpenetration rate. It does not count unique collision pairs, measure severity or responsibility, capture near misses, or predict harm. I use it as a geometric sanity proxy and keep its limitation explicit.

Coach: proxy 不等于 downstream outcome。

What exactly did M7 validate? M7 到底证明了什么？Q11

Bounded answer · English

It verified the selected implementation paths and expected field boundaries on three hand-built cases: position misses velocity-only corruption, fixed-step kinematics misses position-only corruption, and overlap responds to forced interpenetration. Because the audit is outcome-aware and not held out, it is construct and wiring evidence—not calibrated or general evaluator validation.

Coach: field-of-view evidence，不说 validated realism evaluator。

What did the freeze-generator bug teach you? generator artifact 的 insight 是什么？Q12

Bounded answer · English

The original injector changed velocity at the last observed frame, so it erased the future deceleration the kinematic metric should have observed. After preserving history and applying the freeze only in the future, the metric responded. A stress test can teach the evaluator to the answer, so the generator needs its own semantic oracle and immutability checks.

Coach: 这是很好的 failure-and-correction story。

What roles do Waymax and JAX play? 为什么 highlight Waymax/JAX？Q13

Bounded answer · English

Waymax is an optional licensed adapter for WOMD decoding, state construction, and bounded reference comparisons behind EvalSim contracts. The core evaluator can run without Waymax installed, while waymax_kinematic_infeasibility_rate intentionally mirrors one pinned Waymax fixed-0.1-second inverse-bicycle definition. JAX supports bounded jit/vmap computation and a narrow CPU benchmark. Neither technology is ground truth, and their use does not imply a GPU-scale production pipeline.

Coach: technology 是 evidence infrastructure，不是 contribution。

Is this a closed-loop simulator evaluation? 这是闭环实验吗？Q14

Bounded answer · English

The rollout engine supports current-state transitions, and the history-only CV and IDM world-agent paths advance from simulated current state. Privileged log replay is the exception because it copies the recorded future through absolute-state overrides. In accepted M5, ego follows its logged trajectory exogenously for every baseline. I therefore do not claim demonstrated ego-conditioned reactivity; the typed M6 counterfactual path exists pre-data, but no official WOMD outcome is accepted.

Coach: 不用 yes/no oversimplify；拆清 control boundary。

How would you prevent Goodharting? 如何防止模型刷 metric？Q15

Bounded answer · English

I would preserve multiple non-equivalent metrics, version their contracts, pre-register primary gates, retain adverse and null results, and audit controlled defects plus invariances. For a stronger validation claim, I would add source-disjoint held-out defects and real-scene checks. I would also avoid training and selecting a model against exactly the same evaluator suite without a separate decision audit.

Coach: metric plurality + separation + generator audit。

What would you do next? 下一步是什么？Q16

Bounded answer · English

I would start from the missing decision evidence, not the milestone number. If the question is ego-conditioned reactivity, I would complete the separately gated M6 paired study. If the question is general metric validity, I would pre-register source-disjoint calibration and held-out stress cohorts. I would not add GPU or model complexity unless the decision requires it.

Coach: 展示 prioritization，不要回答 “finish every milestone”。

Could you choose CV or IDM for a release decision? 现在能选一个替代另一个吗？Q17

Bounded answer · English

Not from this evidence alone. CV has better logged position and speed fidelity on the fixed cohort, while the IDM–CV overlap and fixed-step kinematic contrasts are not directionally supported. A replacement decision needs a defined use case, reactivity evidence, failure severity, target-distribution slices, and explicit launch thresholds.

Coach: 不回避 decision；列出缺失 evidence。

Do you need Linux or a GPU to reproduce the core story? 环境要求是什么？Q18

Bounded answer · English

No. The contracts, NumPy baselines, metrics, statistics, synthetic construct audit, and presentation are CPU-first. Optional licensed WOMD reproduction requires the pinned TensorFlow, Waymax, and JAX runtime, but the accepted path ran on Apple Silicon CPU. GPU or Linux would need a separate scale or learned-model question.

Coach: 说 core path，不声称 universal portability。

11 · Recall check

Twenty interview flashcards

Say the answer before opening the card. Factual recall should be automatic so your interview bandwidth stays available for reasoning.

1. What is EvalSim’s core question?
核心问题是什么？
Whether distinct diagnostics give one consistent ordering when log replay, CV, and IDM run on the same scenes. Accepted answer: no.
2. What is the contribution?
项目贡献是什么？
The evaluation methodology: explicit constructs, paired comparisons, preserved contradictions, evaluator stress tests, and bounded claims—not a production simulator.
3. What does log replay isolate?
Log replay 的作用？
Exact recorded-future construction and mapping correctness. It is privileged and nonreactive.
4. What does constant velocity isolate?
CV 的作用？
Minimal history-only inertial extrapolation without interaction or map following.
5. What does IDM stand for and do?
IDM 全称及能力？
Intelligent Driver Model; EvalSim uses longitudinal bumper-gap response with no lateral or route-aware controller.
6. What is ego in accepted M5?
M5 中 ego 是什么？
The focal vehicle, copied from the logged trajectory exogenously while policies simulate world agents.
7. Name the four primary metrics.
四个主指标？
position_error_m, speed_error_mps, oriented_box_overlap_rate, and waymax_kinematic_infeasibility_rate.
8. What direction do all four use?
指标方向？
Lower is better, within each metric’s own construct. That does not make their units or meanings interchangeable.
9. What is the accepted cohort?
Cohort 范围？
A fixed deterministic 128-scenario complete-case conditional cohort from WOMD validation shards 00000–00009.
10. Why pair by scenario?
为什么 scenario pairing？
To compare policies under the same scene and avoid scene-mix confounding; agent/frame components are correlated.
11. Position and speed ordering?
位置和速度排序？
Replay < CV < IDM by mean; all are lower-is-better.
12. Overlap ordering and caveat?
Overlap 排序和限制？
Replay < IDM < CV by mean, but the adjusted IDM–CV band crosses zero, so that contrast is not directional.
13. Kinematic ordering and caveat?
Kinematic 排序和限制？
IDM < CV < replay by mean, but IDM–CV is event-sparse with seven nonzero effects and its adjusted band crosses zero.
14. What does 10/12 mean?
10/12 是什么？
Ten of twelve multiplicity-adjusted primary fixed-cohort bands exclude zero and pass the other directional gates. It is not population significance.
15. What are the two unsupported contrasts?
哪两个不能定向？
IDM versus CV for overlap and IDM versus CV for fixed-step kinematic infeasibility.
16. What does M7’s 6+6 mean?
M7 6+6 是什么？
Across four defects and three selected metrics, six cells respond and six show exact non-response over all four doses.
17. What was wrong with freeze v1?
Freeze v1 错在哪里？
It changed velocity at the current observed frame and erased the future deceleration, creating a false kinematic miss.
18. What can M7 not claim?
M7 不能证明什么？
It is not calibrated, source-disjoint, held-out, WOMD-backed, population-level, or general metric validation; speed error was not audited.
19. Why use Waymax and JAX?
Waymax/JAX 做什么？
Waymax supplies optional licensed data/reference adapters; JAX supplies bounded jit/vmap CPU evidence. Neither is ground truth or core-project dependency.
20. What is the one-sentence conclusion?
一句话结论？
Metric choice changes the baseline ordering, so simulator evaluation must remain multi-metric, decision-specific, and explicit about blind spots.

12 · Claim discipline

Do not say this. Say this.

Interview strength comes from matching every sentence to evidence. These substitutions preserve the insight without overselling it.

Do not saySay insteadWhy
“I compared three realistic simulators.”“I used three deliberately simple comparison probes.”Replay is privileged; CV/IDM are limited baselines.
“Replay is ground truth.”“Replay is a privileged recorded-future construction oracle.”It cannot react or provide independent causal truth.
“I found the most realistic simulator.”“The metric families produced conflicting orderings; no overall winner is supported.”No scalar realism construct was validated.
“Overlap measures collisions and safety.”“Overlap is a target-frame geometric interpenetration proxy.”No unique pair count, severity, responsibility, or harm.
“Kinematic infeasibility measures physical realism.”“It is a pinned fixed-0.1-second inverse-dynamics threshold diagnostic.”Not actual-time normalized or complete dynamics.
“The bands are confidence intervals.”“They summarize fixed-cohort sensitivity to empirical scene reweighting.”No probability-sample population inference.
“The 128 scenes represent WOMD.”“They form a deterministic complete-case conditional cohort from ten shards.”No representativeness design.
“M7 validated the evaluators.”“M7 checked selected field-of-view and wiring on three analytic cases.”Outcome-aware, non-held-out evidence.
“M6 proved reactivity.”“M6 has a guarded pre-data implementation but no accepted WOMD outcome.”Implementation is not empirical evidence.
“Waymax verified ground truth.”“Bounded Waymax checks cross-checked overlapping computation semantics.”Shared decode and limited subset prevent independence.
“JAX shows a scalable GPU pipeline.”“JAX ran a bounded batch-two CPU jit/vmap benchmark.”No GPU or end-to-end scaling evidence.
“1,444 tests prove realism.”“Tests support software correctness at the current snapshot.”Test count is not construct validity or generalization.
