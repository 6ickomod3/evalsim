Three baselines · four primary metrics · one fixed 128-scene conditional cohort

The simulator ranking depends on what you measure.

EvalSim asks whether log replay, constant velocity, and the Intelligent Driver Model (IDM) keep the same ordering under recorded-motion fidelity, geometric overlap, and a fixed-0.1-second kinematic-infeasibility diagnostic. Their metric-specific mean orderings differ. In the accepted comparison, the ego—the focal vehicle—is held to its recorded path while each baseline simulates the surrounding world agents. This is a personal learning project and work in progress; the accepted core result and bounded evaluator construct audit are complete, while broader validation remains optional.

01 / Evaluation tension

Realism is not a scalar.

The project starts with deliberately limited policies so their different failure modes remain legible. M5 ran the accepted evaluator on the fixed 128-scenario M4 cohort from exactly ten WOMD validation shards, with pinned Waymax parity checks and paired scene-level statistics. The result is deliberately mixed: every policy contrast has metrics pointing in both directions, so there is no overall winner.

≠

Logged similarity is not reactivity.

Perfect trajectory replay can reproduce a recording exactly while ignoring every counterfactual change made by the ego vehicle.

≠

Low box overlap is not useful motion.

A frozen agent can avoid strict target-frame box interpenetration while being behaviorally useless. This proxy does not measure collision count, severity, safety, or plausibility.

≠

Diversity is not feasibility.

A model can produce varied futures by adding noise—even when those futures leave the road or violate basic kinematics.

03 / Four evaluator questions

Each metric chooses what “better” means.

All four metrics reduce frame- and agent-level components to one scalar per scenario. Lower is better, but the constructs, rewards, and blind spots are different.

Fidelity

How far is the simulated position from the recorded future?

Evaluation design / same scenes first

The comparison unit is the scenario—not an agent or frame.

01 Hold the source fixed

All three baselines run on the same 128 scenarios. Eligibility and slice membership use source data only, before policy outcomes.

02 Reduce within each scene

Every eligible component distribution becomes exactly one scalar per policy × metric × scenario.

03 Compare paired differences

Scene-level A − B effects keep scenario mix fixed; missingness, sparse effects, nulls, and contradictions stay visible.

Small, public, fail-closed API

run_construct_audit() uses exact ConstructAuditCase fixtures and returns an immutable ConstructAuditResult of ConstructAuditCell evidence. construct_audit_defect_registry() isolates FrozenAgentDefectV2 while the historical v1 generator remains unchanged. Invalid or partial metric results, identity/version drift, duplicate or missing cells, non-nested manifests, and incorrect affected-agent counts raise an error instead of becoming apparent misses.

51 focused M7 tests

8 construct-audit, 27 defect, 7 detection, 6 invariance, and 3 governance-card tests. Agent permutation and whole-scene translation must preserve all three selected metrics; rollout-only translation, a velocity impulse, and an independent overlap-geometry oracle prove the checked paths are not vacuous.

What changed

The v1 freeze changed velocity at the observed current frame, erasing the future deceleration. Freeze v2 preserves history and the current frame, then introduces a future-only abrupt stop from the next frame. The old kinematic “miss” was therefore a generator artifact.

What this shows

This outcome-aware audit used exactly three hand-built analytic cases at four doses (0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00) and produced six responses and six non-responses. Position reads coordinates, overlap reads interpenetration, and the fixed-step kinematic diagnostic reads stored state transitions. The corrected freeze response is evidence about an abrupt-stop discontinuity, not generic nonreactivity.

What this does not show

The field-separated non-responses are partly true by construction. This outcome-aware evidence is not calibrated, source-disjoint, held-out, WOMD-backed, population-level, or general metric validation. speed_error_mps is excluded from this audit; it supports no aggregate realism score, metric winner, or simulator winner.

Completed, bounded construct audit only. The original M7 v1 gates remain unmet, and broader validation is optional and separately gated.

05 / Advanced engineering evidence

The engine owns the physics boundary.

Policies propose typed state overrides or controls. The engine owns time, identity, lifecycle, feasibility integration, and the evidence trail.

Open the rollout transition, actor-control boundary, and provenance shape

  2. Observe nowBuild immutable current-step frames; CV and IDM are tested against poisoned future world kinematics.
  3. Policy stepReturn typed controls or explicit absolute-state overrides.
  4. IntegrateApply actual Δt and audited acceleration, speed, braking, and yaw clamps.
  5. Update togetherSynchronously advance world agents; preserve births and re-entries.
  6. Record proofEmit rollout state plus complete engine and policy provenance.

One contract, two kinds of actor control

In the accepted M5 comparison, ego remains exogenous and follows its logged trajectory while policies simulate world agents from the current state. What we do not know—yet.

  • The M4 cohort is conditional, not representative. It is a deterministic complete-case sample from exactly ten validation shards, not a random sample, the full dataset, or evidence of population-level generalization.
  • The decode boundary is shared. EvalSim and the Waymax reference paths use the same pinned Waymax WOMD decoder, so their agreement is not fully independent source validation.
  • M5 is finite-cohort diagnostic evidence, not a winner. The accepted result is conditional on one deterministic 128-scenario complete-case cohort, shared Waymax decode, seed-zero policies, logged/exogenous ego motion, and a privileged recorded-future replay baseline. Every contrast has diagnostics in both directions; there is no overall realism, superiority, causal, production, or population-level claim.
  • The uncertainty language is intentionally narrow. The pointwise and adjusted ranges are deterministic scene-reweighting sensitivity bands, not confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, or population uncertainty. The overlap rate is a target-frame proxy, and the kinematic rate is a fixed-0.1-second Waymax diagnostic rather than a physical-time-normalized safety metric. Native offroad, route, and signal metrics remain outside the current contract.
  • The benchmark is deliberately narrow. It measures a batch-2 exact-log JAX kernel on Apple CPU after host-to-device transfer—not ingestion, the full cohort, accelerator speedup, scaling efficiency, or production throughput. RSS is process high-water memory, not JAX device memory.
  • M6 is implemented pre-data, not scientifically accepted. The typed ego-perturbation seam, paired reactivity metrics, and outcome-blind runner exist, but no WOMD evaluation has run. Because the strategy change updates a source-catalogued file, fresh exact-source review and approval are required before official M6 data access.
  • M7 construct evidence is not general evaluator validation. The completed outcome-aware audit covers three hand-built analytic cases, four controlled defects, three metrics, and four doses. The historical v1 freeze changed velocity at the current frame and erased future deceleration; corrected freeze v2 preserves that boundary and creates a future-only abrupt stop. Its kinematic response therefore demonstrates sensitivity to that discontinuity, not generic nonreactivity. The matrix is exactly six responses and six non-responses; original v1 gates remain unmet, and broader validation is optional and separately gated.

Run the implemented paths.

The core explanation, synthetic checks, and bounded M7 construct audit run in the repository’s CPU-first uv-managed environment without Linux or a GPU. Waymax/JAX/WOMD are not required for that data-free path; they are required for separately licensed M3–M5 real-data/reference reproduction and any separately approved official M6 real-data execution. The official ten-shard M5 run produced the accepted aggregate matrix shown above; its raw data, detailed diagnostics, and generated result store remain ignored and local-only.

WOMD data and all derived artifacts are excluded from Git and deployment. M3 requires separately obtained shard 00000; M4 and M5 require the exact ten-shard set 00000–00009, the accepted ignored M4 run, a clean pushed worktree, a fresh ignored output directory, explicit data-access opt-in, and acceptance of the complete upstream terms above.
